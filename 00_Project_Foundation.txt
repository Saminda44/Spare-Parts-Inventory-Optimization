# 1. Purpose

This document defines the engineering foundation for the Enterprise Spare Parts Intelligence Platform.

The platform is designed for Yamaha spare parts procurement in a B2B environment.

The objective is to transform raw business data and Yamaha Parts Catalogues into intelligent procurement decisions using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Operations Research, Optimization and Explainable AI.

This document defines every engineering standard that every later module must follow.

No intelligence module should violate the rules defined here.

The spare parts business operates under a Business-to-Business (B2B) model.

# 13. Business Assumptions

• Historical dealer purchases approximate market demand.

• UIO represents the installed vehicle population.

• Compatible UIO influences spare part demand.

• Dealers exhibit repeatable purchasing behaviour.

• Vehicle age influences replacement demand.

• Lead time uncertainty affects safety stock.

• Catalogue compatibility is assumed correct unless verified otherwise.
