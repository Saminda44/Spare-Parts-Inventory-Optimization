# 13. Enterprise Data Ownership

One of the biggest problems in enterprise AI systems is unclear ownership.

Every dataset in the platform must have exactly one owner.

Other services may READ the data.

Only the owner service may WRITE the data.

Example

Catalogue Service
    owns
        SKU Master

Vehicle Service
    owns
        Vehicle Forecast

UIO Service
    owns
        UIO Forecast

Dealer Service
    owns
        Dealer Features

Demand Service
    owns
        Demand Forecast

Inventory Service
    owns
        Safety Stock

Procurement Service
    owns
        Procurement Plan

Decision Service
    owns
        Final Recommendation

This prevents conflicting updates.

# 30. End-to-End Execution Sequence

1. Load new business data.
2. Validate all datasets.
3. Update master data.
4. Run Catalogue Intelligence (if new catalogues exist).
5. Refresh Knowledge Layer.
6. Execute Vehicle Intelligence.
7. Execute UIO Intelligence.
8. Update Product Intelligence.
9. Update Dealer Intelligence.
10. Execute Demand Intelligence.
11. Execute Forecast Intelligence.
12. Execute Inventory Intelligence.
13. Run Procurement Optimization.
14. Generate Decision Support outputs.
15. Publish dashboards.
16. Store audit records.
17. Monitor results.
18. Compare forecasts with actuals.
19. Trigger continuous learning if performance thresholds are exceeded.

This becomes the standard monthly operational workflow.
