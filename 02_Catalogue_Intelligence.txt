Module 02

Catalogue Intelligence

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

Catalogue Intelligence converts raw Yamaha spare-parts PDF catalogues into an enterprise-quality SKU Master and Compatibility Knowledge Base.

The module is responsible for understanding every catalogue and generating structured business knowledge that can be consumed by downstream intelligence services.

Catalogue Intelligence is the foundation of the entire Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform.

Without a correct SKU Master, all forecasting, demand estimation, inventory optimization, and procurement recommendations become unreliable.

---

# Business Objectives

The module shall

• Discover catalogues

• Understand catalogue structure

• Extract all spare parts

• Identify compatible motorcycle models

• Extract colour variants

• Generate a normalized SKU Master

• Populate the Knowledge Graph

• Produce confidence scores

• Route uncertain records for review

• Continuously improve through feedback

# 02_03_OCR_Intelligence.md

Module 02.03

OCR Intelligence Engine

Version 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The OCR Intelligence Engine converts optimized catalogue pages into structured,
coordinate-aware textual information while preserving document semantics.

Unlike conventional OCR systems, this module understands the visual structure
of spare-parts catalogues and prepares structured information for downstream
semantic extraction.

OCR Intelligence does not directly extract spare parts.

Its responsibility is to accurately recognize all textual elements and preserve
their spatial relationships.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Motorcycle spare-parts catalogues contain

• Tables

• Remarks

• Callout numbers

• Model names

• Colour abbreviations

• Footnotes

• Headers

• Exploded diagrams

Traditional OCR often

• breaks rows

• merges columns

• loses coordinates

• ignores superscripts

• confuses similar characters

• destroys reading order

This causes downstream extraction failures.

---

# 3. Objectives

The engine shall

✓ Recognize all text

✓ Preserve coordinates

✓ Preserve reading order

✓ Preserve page hierarchy

✓ Generate confidence

✓ Support multiple OCR engines
