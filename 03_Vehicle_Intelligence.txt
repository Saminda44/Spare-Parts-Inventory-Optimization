# 03_06_UIO_Intelligence.md

Enterprise Spare Parts Intelligence Platform

Phase 03

Module 03.06

UIO Intelligence

Version 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

UIO Intelligence estimates and forecasts the future Units In Operation (UIO)
for every motorcycle model.

UIO represents the active motorcycle population currently operating in the
market.

Unlike cumulative sales calculations,
this module continuously estimates

• New vehicle additions

• Vehicle retirements

• Survival probabilities

• Fleet ageing

• Regional fleet distribution

• Dealer fleet distribution

• Scenario-based fleet evolution

The resulting UIO becomes the primary driver of spare-parts demand forecasting.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Spare-part demand depends on

ACTIVE motorcycles

not

historical motorcycle sales.

Example

Sales

2018

1000

2019

1200

2020

800

2021

500

Production ends

2022

↓

Many motorcycles remain in service

↓

Parts demand continues
