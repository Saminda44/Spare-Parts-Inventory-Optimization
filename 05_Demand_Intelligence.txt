Demand Master Intelligence

---

# 1. Purpose

Demand Master Intelligence creates a centralized enterprise definition for every
SKU from a demand-planning perspective.

Instead of treating spare parts as simple inventory items,
this module transforms every SKU into an intelligent planning entity by
combining catalogue knowledge, vehicle compatibility, fleet intelligence,
dealer behaviour, lifecycle, business rules and historical demand.

Demand Master becomes the primary reference for every downstream forecasting,
inventory and procurement service.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Traditional ERP systems only store

• Part Number

• Description

• Price

• Warehouse

These fields are insufficient for intelligent forecasting.

A planner also needs to know

• Which vehicles consume the part

• How many vehicles exist

• Vehicle age

• Fleet growth

• Replacement probability

• Colour dependency

• Dealer demand behaviour

• Criticality

• Demand pattern

• Lead time

• Forecastability

Therefore a Demand Master must be built.

# 1. Purpose

Demand Validation and Governance ensures that every demand intelligence
artifact meets enterprise quality, consistency, explainability and governance
requirements before publication.

The module validates forecasts, uncertainty estimates, scenarios, feature
quality, digital twins and knowledge graph consistency.

Only validated outputs are released to downstream enterprise services.
