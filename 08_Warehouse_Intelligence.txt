# 1. Purpose

Autonomous Warehouse Intelligence enables AI-driven warehouse operations
by continuously optimizing storage, movement, labor, equipment and
operational decisions while ensuring governance, explainability and
human oversight.

Instead of requiring manual warehouse planning,
the engine autonomously manages warehouse operations within approved
business policies.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Warehouse operations involve thousands of repetitive decisions every day.

Manual planning often results in

Slow Response

Inefficient Resource Allocation

Operational Bottlenecks

High Labor Costs

Inconsistent Decisions

Reactive Warehouse Management
